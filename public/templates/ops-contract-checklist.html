Contract Checklist

Date: [DD/MM/YYYY] · Contract: [Contract title]
Before you sign: Go through this checklist to make sure the contract covers all the key clauses that protect your business. Cross off items that are included; flag any missing ones before signing.

1. Parties & Scope

  • Full legal names of both parties are correctly spelled
  • Physical addresses and contact details included
  • Scope of work / deliverables are clearly described
  • Timeline with milestones and final delivery date
  • Acceptance criteria defined (what counts as "done")

2. Money & Payment

  • Total amount and currency (GH₵) stated clearly
  • Payment schedule (deposit, milestones, final payment)
  • Late payment penalties or interest explained
  • Invoicing process defined (who sends, when, payment methods)
  • Tax responsibilities stated (VAT, withholding tax)

3. Rights & Ownership

  • Intellectual property (IP) ownership defined
  • Confidentiality / non-disclosure clause included
  • Limitation of liability (what each party is responsible for)
  • Indemnification clause (who covers losses from third-party claims)
  • Force majeure clause (unforeseeable events like floods, strikes)

4. Termination & Disputes

  • Termination conditions (how and when either party can end it)
  • Notice period required before termination
  • Penalties for early termination
  • Dispute resolution process (mediation, arbitration, court)
  • Governing law (Ghana law or other)

5. Signatures & Attachments

  • Signature lines with dates for both parties
  • Witness signatures (if required)
  • Schedules / annexures attached (Scope, Timeline, Pricing)
  • Both parties hold an original signed copy
  • Company stamp or chop applied (if applicable)
Tip: If any item is missing, raise it before signing. A signed contract is legally binding — don't assume things will be added later verbally.
