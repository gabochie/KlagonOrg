# CCC-AI-01 — AI Literacy: Working With AI

- Home: CCC / Digital · Level L0 · 3 weeks · 6 lessons · Core · pilot
- Prerequisites: SOT-DIG-01 or equivalent. Free-tier AI tools only.
- Why: durable human-AI collaboration before tool courses; ethics + verification built in.

## Outcomes
1. Explain what generative AI can/can't do, including hallucinations.
2. Write, iterate and verify prompts for research, drafting, summarising.
3. Apply disclosure, privacy and ethics (no sensitive data, cite AI use).
4. Produce a verified AI-assisted brief usable in SOT-AI-01.

## Skills
ai-literacy, critical-thinking, research

## Lessons
1. **What AI Is (and Isn't)** — capabilities, limits, hallucinations, bias. Do: get AI to err on local facts, document the error. Evidence: error log.
2. **Prompting Basics** — role, context, constraints, format, examples. Do: rewrite one vague prompt 3 ways, compare outputs. Evidence: prompt trio.
3. **AI for Research** — search vs generate, cross-checking, sources. Do: research a Ghanaian topic (e.g. agribusiness inputs), verify 3 claims. Evidence: source table.
4. **AI for Writing & Summarising** — outlines, drafts, plain language, editing (not copy-paste). Do: draft + human-edited community notice. Evidence: before/after.
5. **Privacy, Ethics & Disclosure** — what never to paste (MoMo PINs, IDs, client data), disclosure labels, academic honesty. Do: redact a risky prompt, write disclosure line. Evidence: ethics checklist.
6. **My AI Workflow** — pick one real workflow (study, work, business), build a repeatable prompt template. Do: template + one run + verification note. Evidence: template doc.

## Final project
Verified AI-assisted research brief (2 pages): question, method, AI outputs, human verification, sources, disclosure statement.

## Assessment
Pass = brief with ≥3 verified sources + disclosure + ethics quiz ≥70%. Passport: `ai-literacy — Demonstrated`. Feeds directly into SOT-AI-01.
