# SOA-PHO-01 — Mobile Photography & Visual Storytelling

- School: SOA / Photography · Level L1 · 4 weeks · 6 lessons · Elective · pilot
- Prerequisites: none. Any camera phone. Consent-first.
- Why: phone-only storytelling feeding Art, community documentation, portfolios.

## Outcomes
1. Shoot with intent (light, composition, moment) on a phone.
2. Edit consistently on mobile (exposure, crop, colour) without over-processing.
3. Sequence a 10-image story with captions and consent log.
4. Publish for WhatsApp, print, and portfolio.

## Skills
photography, visual-storytelling, visual-literacy

## Lessons
1. **Light & Seeing** — golden hour, shade, window light; clean backgrounds. Do: same subject in 3 lights. Evidence: trio.
2. **Composition on a Phone** — rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, 10-shot drill. Do: 10 distinct frames of one place. Evidence: contact sheet.
3. **People & Consent** — ask, explain use, minors/elderly care, model release by message/note. Do: 3 consented portraits + log. Evidence: portraits + log (no private data in submission beyond consent record).
4. **Community Story** — theme, shot list (wide/medium/detail/portrait/action), captions. Do: draft 10-image plan for your community story. Evidence: shot list.
5. **Edit on Mobile** — crop/straighten, exposure, colour consistency, export presets. Do: edit the story to one consistent look. Evidence: before/after pair.
6. **Sequence & Publish** — order, titles, captions, WhatsApp vs print sizing. Do: publish story page/PDF. Evidence: final story.

## Final project
10-image documented community story with captions + consent log (Ghana lens: trade, craft, food, faith, transport, neighbourhood change, cultural preservation).

## Assessment
Rubric: story coherence (30), technical control (25), ethics/consent (25), captions/sequence (20). Pass ≥60. Passport: `visual-storytelling — Demonstrated`. Doubles as P3 portfolio piece.
