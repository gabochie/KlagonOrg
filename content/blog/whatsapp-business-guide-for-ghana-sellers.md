# WhatsApp Business for Ghana Sellers: A Complete Starter Guide

In Ghana, WhatsApp *is* the mall. Your customers are already there — checking statuses between orders of banku, forwarding deals to their families, asking traders "wo de 3 wo sem 3ni?"

Yet most small sellers use WhatsApp like a private chat: no catalog, no quick replies, no organized orders. They're working in a shop built with 80% of its shelves unused.

This guide walks you through setting up WhatsApp Business properly — catalog, labels, quick replies, and a simple order system — so your craft, food, or wholesale business stops losing sales to miss-calls and forgotten messages.

## Why WhatsApp Business, not regular WhatsApp

The free WhatsApp Business app exists for one reason: small sellers like you. It gives you:

- **A business profile** — your shop name, logo, address, and hours, so strangers trust you faster.
- **A catalog** — a mini online shop inside WhatsApp. Customers scroll your products and prices without asking "how much?" for the hundredth time.
- **Labeling** — tag customers as "new", "returning", "to call back", "order pending". No more "seen's and forgetting".
- **Quick replies** — type `/hours` to instantly answer "when do you open?" Common questions become button presses.
- **Away message** — automatic reply when you're busy frying, selling, or sleeping.

Regular WhatsApp is a phone number. WhatsApp Business is a shop with a front door.

## Step-by-step setup (10 minutes)

1. **Download** the WhatsApp Business app from Play Store or App Store.
2. **Verify** your number. It can be your personal number — the app runs separately from your normal WhatsApp.
3. **Build your profile:** business name, category (e.g., "Bakery", "Fashion & Jewellery", "Local Food"), logo photo, description ("Name the dish, we deliver anywhere in Klagon"). Add your exact location and hours.
4. **Create your catalog.** Start with just 3–5 best-sellers — not everything. Each item needs a clear photo, price, and one-line description. An accepted-in-advance price kills the "how much" barrier.
5. **Set your away message and quick replies.** Write the three most-asked questions and their answers: prices/delivery, location, order process.
6. **Save the number.** Share your WhatsApp Business number everywhere — that's now your order line.

## A simple order system that prevents chaos

The seller who loses money on WhatsApp is the one who handles orders in their head. Use **labels** and a **note** per order:

- When someone asks for a product, tag them **"order-pending"**.
- Check pending labels twice a day (morning and evening).
- When payment arrives → tag **"paid"**. When delivered → tag **"done"**.
- Every Sunday, write a small list: which items sold this week, which didn't. That list is your stock bible.

This looks obvious, but it's the difference between "I sold a lot, but I don't know what I earned" and knowing your numbers. And your numbers are the key to growing.

## Status updates that actually sell

You probably already post statuses. The sellers who *grow* follow three rules:

1. **Value before product.** One educational status per day — "3 ways to store banku overnight" — builds the trust that makes the second status ("new delivery tomorrow 6am") convert.
2. **Proof sells.** Post actual deliver-and-paid screenshots (with customers' consent). "Yesterday's orders ✅" beats any advert.
3. **A call-to-action.** End statuses with something to do: "WhatsApp me 'BEEF' to reserve yours." Asking directly converts passive viewers into orders.

And never spam-broadcast every contact. Use **recent chat** and **labels** to target. A status or broadcast to people who already buy from you is a personal invitation; a broadcast to everyone you've ever met is begging.

## Growing trust in a small market

Ghanaian buyers transact on trust. Reinforce it cheaply:

- Respond within minutes during business hours — response speed is your cheapest marketing.
- Confirm orders back in writing: product, quantity, price, delivery time. Written confirmation prevents the "I said a different size" argument.
- Never post others' order details or money claims unless you have permission.
- Take a WhatsApp Business **"checkout"/payment confirmation** if you have it available in your region; otherwise confirm mobile-money references and keep a simple sales column in your notebook or a free spreadsheet app.

## Start this week

WhatsApp Business takes ten minutes to set up and a week to turn into a habit. If you've been selling through regular WhatsApp, switch this weekend: build your profile, add five items to the catalog, write three quick replies, and label your pending orders.
