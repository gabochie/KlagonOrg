# Klagon Street Food Guide (2026): The Taste of Klagon Food Walk

One plate, one family, one story. The Taste of Klagon food walk is the edible episode of the [Discover Klagon series](/visit/walk/): shop the 5:45am market with your food host, cook (or watch) in a resident kitchen, and eat with the family that fed you.

## The plates

- **Kenkey** — fermented corn, steamed in its husk, sour-sweet, eaten with fried fish and pepper. Sourdough with a birth certificate.
- **Banku** — fermented corn-cassava dough, smooth, served with soup or okro and fish.
- **Waakye** — the rice-and-beans morning classic with spaghetti, gari, egg, fish and stew.
- **Kpokpoi** — the festival dish: steamed fermented corn with palm soup, sprinkled for ancestors at Homowo and eaten by the family after.

These are the staples that anchor Klagon's food lane — the same lanes this series' [market guide](/blog/klagon-market-guide/) walks you through at dawn.

## The walk

The arc is simple: **market shop → kitchen → table.** Your host picks the ingredients for your meal, the family eats with you, and the closing [Impact Bill](/visit/walk/) names the hosts who fed you. Specific vendor names are confirmed at booking, and dietary needs go to the concierge ahead of the day.

## Practical notes

- Runs as its own edition — book via WhatsApp from the [walk page](/visit/walk/).
- Vegetarian and allergy-friendly menus are arranged in advance.
- For sit-down restaurant picks across the corridor, see [where to eat near Klagon](/blog/where-to-eat-near-klagon/).
