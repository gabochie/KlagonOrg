# Klagon by Night (2026): Grill, Music & the After-Dark Walk

The town does not sleep when the market closes — it changes shift. Gibson's fires the grill, weekend live music rolls through, night kitchens steam, and the corridor glows toward VK City. Klagon by Night is the town exhaling.

## The evening arc

Golden hour at the lagoon first, with the cormorants coming home. Then dinner where the town dines: grilled fish, banku, and pepper that means business. Gibson's weekend sets anchor the night, Dylex Bistro and Sizzle Grill cover the sit-down lane, and VK City takes the late shift.

## The walk

**Klagon by Night** runs in the evening at GH₵450 (confirm at booking): night kitchen tastings, a live-music stop, and the lit-up corridor story — estates glowing where hyena hills used to be dark. Small groups, guide throughout, concierge on WhatsApp. It rides the precedent that structured night tourism works: GTDC's Accra-by-Night moved 740+ passengers in eight months — Klagon does it at street level.

## Ground rules

Evenings need the same consent discipline as mornings: ask before close-ups, stay with the group, and confirm ride-hail pickup points in advance. Dark streets, bright kitchens — your guide knows both.
