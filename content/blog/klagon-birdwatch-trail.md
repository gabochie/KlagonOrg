# Klagon Birdwatch Trail (2026): Dawn Birding at Sakumo Lagoon

5:30. Flat water, first light, reeds going gold. The stilts are already at work, a spotted redshank lifts off, and somewhere in the shallows the black heron spreads his wings into a hunting canopy. You are out with the people who count these birds as family — and they have been counting for years.

## The morning

Binoculars are provided and your guide whispers the IDs as each bird arrives: black-winged stilts picking the shallows, little stints fresh off migration, cormorants drying their wings, and with luck the heron himself. The counters' clipboards are real data — annual counts feed the BirdLife IBA record. You are not just watching; the morning's list joins the town's ledger.

## Why Sakumo

Sakumo is a Ramsar wetland hosting 70+ species and around 30,000 birds in the season, with surrounding communities running annual counts in partnership with FORS. The [Klagon map](/map/) shows the lagoon's edge and the corridors that reach it.

## Practical details

- **Start:** 5:30am, lagoon edge meet — the concierge confirms the exact pin on WhatsApp.
- **Cost:** GH₵350 (confirm at booking), binoculars included.
- **Bring:** hat, light jacket, closed shoes that can take mud, charged phone.
- **Provided:** binoculars, your guide, and the silence.
- **Friday mornings** carry the no-fishing-till-noon pact — the most reverent dawn of the week.

## Extending it

Specialist birders already reach Sakumono through operators; this is the community layer on top — breakfast from the market after, the full **Wetland & Market Walk** on Saturday, and the [Impact Bill](/visit/walk/) at the end. Adventure birding brings you to the doorstep; the town takes you inside.
