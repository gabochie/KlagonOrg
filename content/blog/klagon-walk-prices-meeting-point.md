# Klagon Walk Prices & Meeting Point (2026): What Your Ticket Covers

Discover Klagon is the community-narrated tour of Klagon, Tema West — led and paid residents, small groups, and every cedi accounted for in an Impact Bill. This is the one page that shows you what the walks cost, where you meet, how booking works, and where the money goes. Prices and times are as of September 2026 — confirm the current terms when you book.

## The three signature walks

- **The Wetland & Market Walk** — 4 hours, GH₵750 per person, groups of 4–10. The route out of Shalom Junction takes in the palace name story, Klagon Market, a resident kitchen, the 1965 cattle underpass, factory street, and the Sakumo lagoon at golden hour — closing with a meal and your Impact Bill.
- **Dawn Birding** — starts 5:30, GH₵350. First light over the Ramsar wetland with a resident guide; binoculars provided.
- **Klagon by Night** — evenings, GH₵450. Night kitchens, street life and a live-music stop with guides who live here.

The **Homowo edition** is seasonal — dates and price are announced on release, and it books over WhatsApp only. Groups of eight or more get a group rate, and schools, churches and corporate groups are welcome with consent and transport handled.

## Where to meet

Every walk meets at **Shalom Junction**, the Lashibi–Klagon boundary, at the confirmed start time. After booking, the concierge sends a live WhatsApp pin so there is no guesswork about the day in question.

## How booking works

The booking flow is one WhatsApp conversation: tap **Book a Walk** on the tour page, send a 20% deposit, and the concierge confirms your meeting point, transport, dietary needs and the photo policy. After the walk you get the Impact Bill and a chance to review. Everything happens on [the Discover Klagon walk page](/visit/walk/).

## Where your ticket goes

Tourism that gives back is a receipt, not a slogan. Each walk closes with a printed Impact Bill that names where the cedi went: the food hosts and mapped businesses, your resident guide, the Klagon Community Fund, and operations. You can see the split on the [walk page](/visit/walk/) and explore the places behind it on the [Klagon map](/map/).
