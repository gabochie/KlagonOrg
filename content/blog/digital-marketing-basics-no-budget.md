# Digital Marketing Basics: Promote Anything Without a Big Budget

"Marketing needs money." It's repeated so often it feels like a law of physics.

But watch any successful small seller in Ghana and you'll see the reality: **the best marketing tools are free, and the big tool is hustle, not cash.**

Here are the digital marketing basics that work starting at zero cedis — for a business, a service, a church, or a cause.

## The 6 free channels that actually move things

### 1. WhatsApp status & broadcast lists (your #1 weapon)
In Ghana, WhatsApp *is* the internet for many people. A business that posts genuinely useful statuses and keeps organised broadcast lists is already most of the way to promoted.

Creator's tip: don't broadcast spam daily. **Broadcast value:** tips, offers, real stories. Two to three quality statuses a week beats nine ad-like ones.

### 2. Organic social: pick ONE platform and go deep
Fight the urge to be everywhere. Choose the one platform your actual customers use (Instagram for fashion/beauty, TikTok for youth products, Facebook for community groups), post consistently for 60 days, and let the algorithm find you.

### 3. Google Business Profile (free local machine)
If you serve your local area — add your business to Google Maps. People searching "tailor near me" or "repair shop [your area]" will find you. It costs nothing and lasts for years.

### 4. Groups and communities
Join relevant WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups, church and alumni pages — then *contribute*, don't dump ads. Answer questions. Share helpful posts. The reputation you build converts to customers who already trust you.

### 5. Referrals you actually ask for
Every happy customer is a billboard. The step everyone skips is *asking*: "If you know anyone who needs this, please send them my way." Simple, free, and compounding.

### 6. Simple content that answers questions
A one-minute video answering a common customer question ("How do I clean this?") gets shared and searched forever. Useful content is free advertising with a long lifespan.

## Building your weekly rhythm

Consistency beats volume. Here's a sustainable weekly plan:

| Day | 20-minute action |
|---|---|
| Mon | Post one useful tip (status/story) |
| Wed | Post one real customer story/testimonial |
| Fri | Offer or announcement |
| Weekend | Reply as fast as you can to every message |

One consistent hour a week, for two months — that's what gets results. Not one hero campaign in March.

## The mental shift that matters most

Almost everyone new to marketing thinks about *what they want to say.* The professionals think about **what their customer needs to hear.**

- "I sell phones" (you-centric) vs "Phones that survive your hustle" (them-centric)
- "Great tailoring services" (you-centric) vs "Clothes that fit right, made in 3 days" (them-centric)

Adopt the customer's view and even your cheap posts start landing.

## Avoid the 3 money-wasters

1. **Buying followers.** They don't buy products. Organic, engaged, small beats purchased and empty.
2. **Posting everywhere at once.** Thin presence on five platforms loses to depth on one.
3. **Paying for ads before your basics work.** If your statuses and referrals haven't found traction yet, ads will waste the money. Master free first.
